How does Upslope measure altitude?

Your iPhone's barometer measures air pressure very precisely, so it tracks every metre you climb, even in airplane mode. GPS sets the starting level. Next to the number, Upslope shows which source it uses and how accurate it is right now.

Why does it say ±30 m indoors?

GPS is weak indoors, so the starting level is less certain. Step outside for a minute and the range narrows. You can also tap the altitude and type a known value from a trail sign or summit marker.

When does the pressure trend appear?

After about 30 minutes with the app open. The pressure-drop alert needs about 2 hours of readings, and it only works while Upslope is open, including during a recorded hike.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Upslope. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

I bought Pro but it is not active

Open the Pro screen in Upslope and tap "Restore purchases", using the same Apple ID you bought with.
