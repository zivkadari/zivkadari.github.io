Upslope
Altimeter & Barometer

Your altitude, even with no signal.

Upslope reads your iPhone's barometer, so it works in airplane mode and deep in the mountains. It shows where the number comes from and how accurate it is.

  • Live altitude and air pressure, free.
  • Hike log: total ascent, highest point, altitude profile.
  • Pressure trend and an alert when a storm may be coming.
  • No ads, no tracking, no account.
