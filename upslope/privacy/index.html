Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Upslope is an altimeter and barometer app. We built it to work on your phone, without an account and without tracking. This policy explains the little data the app uses.

What stays on your phone

  • Air pressure and altitude from your iPhone's barometer, your pressure history and your recorded hikes are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Location: Upslope uses your location only while the app is open, to set your starting altitude from GPS and to show your coordinates and today's sunset time. Your location is not stored with your hikes, not sent to us and not shared with anyone. It leaves your phone only if you tap your position and share it yourself.
  • Notifications (pressure-drop alert, trial reminder) are created on your device. No push service is used.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history from Apple and basic device information (such as the app version and country). It also receives a few anonymous usage events (for example, that onboarding was finished or the subscription screen was shown), which help us improve the app. None of this includes your name, email, location or altitude data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

We do not receive your payment details; Apple handles payment.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data with data brokers.

Children

Upslope is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
